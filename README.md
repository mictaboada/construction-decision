# Stop-Work Call: Heat on a Construction Site

An interactive page on whether a construction company should send workers out in extreme heat.

**Live site:** https://mictaboada.github.io/construction-decision/

## Group members

- Michelle Taboada
- Jonathan Silva
- Elysse Hall

## What it shows

Sliders for heat (the hazard) and for how many workers are new to working in heat (the vulnerability) combine to drive the result. A livability map shows how hard a healthy adult aged 18 to 40 can work in the weather without heat building up in the body. Sources for every number are listed on the page.
